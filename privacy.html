Privacy policy

Last updated: 25 October 2026

Nowsplained Uploader is a private tool used only by the owner of the Nowsplained channel to save its own videos and documents to the owner's Google Drive.

What it accesses

It uses the Google Drive permission drive.file, which only allows access to files the tool itself created. It cannot see any other files in the Drive.

What it does with data

It uploads video and document files that the owner produced, and can share a file by link so a scheduling service can post it. It does not collect, sell or share personal information, and no other people use it.

Removing access

Access can be removed at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
